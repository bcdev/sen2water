Sen2Water - Overview

Introduction

Sentinel-2 MSI pixel identification and atmospheric correction over ocean and inland water.
Sen2Water processes Sentinel-2 MSI L1C granules with top-of-atmosphere reflectances. The input is expected in SAFE format, unpacked in a directory. The 13 input bands of 10 m, 20 m, and 60 m resolution are processed into water-leaving reflectances over water areas at 60 m resolution. In addition, flags are provided in three flag mask variables. The output data format is NetCDF4 with internal compression. The water-leaving reflectances are either calculated by POLYMER, ACOLITE, or C2RCC depending on which one is best suited for the location. In addition, Idepix is used to calculate corresponding flags.
